The Tomorrow Method

You can't predict the future.
You can be ready for every version of it.

For fifty years, corporations have used scenario planning to prepare for futures they couldn't forecast — building several plausible tomorrows and stress-testing every plan against the lot. The Tomorrow Method is that discipline, walked down from the executive floor and handed to the people those plans are usually about.

One method, three lands

Each book points the same engine at a different kind of unpredictable weather. Same grammar throughout: you never predict, you prepare; every future gets a plan; and the plan is one you made yourself.
